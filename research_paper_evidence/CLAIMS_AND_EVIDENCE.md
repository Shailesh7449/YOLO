## 2. Summary of Manuscript Writing Directives

When drafting the IEEE research paper in Claude:

1. **Use Claims Marked "YES (Safe)"**: These represent verified source implementations, physical assets, or live runtime tests.
2. **Use Claims Marked "QUALIFIED ONLY" with Clear Caveats**: Clearly state that throughput ($11-21\text{ FPS}$), alert latency ($< 850\text{ ms}$), and GFLOPs ($\sim 8.2-8.5$) are **theoretical computational estimates** derived from algorithmic operation counts, with empirical benchmarking labeled as **Pending controlled experiment**.
3. **Completely Omit Claims Marked "NO (Unsafe)"**: Do not include fictional model names ("YOLO26"), unverified state-of-the-art accuracy claims, false dataset labels (stampedes in UCSD Ped2), or non-existent GAT/MMAction2 modules.
